A lot on your mind? Start with one small next step.

InnenFlow gives you a short space to reflect. Choose how you feel, name what is taking up space, and pick one manageable next step. Try your first reset without creating an account.

YOUR TWO-MINUTE RESET
Choose a topic such as studies, work or relationships. Write a thought if you want to, or skip the details. Finish with a next step you choose yourself. Sign in to keep your reset in your private journal.

A JOURNAL THAT FITS YOUR DAY
Keep a brief reflection or write a longer entry. Record your mood, habits and daily check-in. Optional voice input lets you dictate instead of typing when your device supports it.

SEE YOUR WEEK
Look back at the days you checked in and the topics that came up in your resets. See the last next step you chose. These are observations from your own entries, not a diagnosis or a promise about how you should feel.

MORE WAYS TO PAUSE
Explore meditation timers, focus activities, small wins, articles and practical wisdom. Start with the reset and explore the rest when you want to.

SIX SIMPLE PROFILE PICTURES
Choose a ready-made portrait. No photo upload or character builder is needed.

YOUR CHOICES MATTER
Your personal journal is visible to your own account. Posting to the anonymous board is a separate, optional action. Anonymous board posts expire after 30 days. Notifications are optional and can be enabled from Profile. Manage your account and access account deletion from Profile.

Unsaved guest reset words stay only in the current app session. Sign-in and saved journal features require an internet connection.

InnenFlow is for personal reflection and everyday wellbeing. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency services.
